# Multiverse PhilBill — Product Specification

## 1. Problem

Millions of Filipinos carry legitimate unpaid bills and financial pressure.  
At the same time, skilled content creators and kinetic operators are blocked by missing tools that would clearly raise their output and skill ceiling.

Traditional charity is slow, bureaucratic, and often feels extractive.  
Social media “gift me” posts are mostly ignored because there is no proof of capability or clear signal.

## 2. Solution

A swipe-based platform where people upload real financial needs or high-signal level-up tools together with a short, honest story of the choice-point that created their current situation.

Donors swipe based on resonance, not guilt.

## 3. Primary User Types

### Receiver
- Has a real unpaid bill, shopping list, or specific high-value tool they need
- Can tell a short, truthful story about why this exists in their timeline
- For Creator Level-Up items: must demonstrate existing skill in that domain

### Donor
- Has excess liquidity (local high-net-worth, diaspora, or simply someone who wants direct impact)
- Prefers speed, clarity, and personal resonance over institutional charity
- Wants the option to stay anonymous or claim the action

## 4. Core User Flows

### Receiver Flow
1. Create account (phone or Google)
2. Upload photo of actual bill / item
3. Enter amount + category
4. Write short story (or record voice note)
5. Submit → appears in feed

### Donor Flow
1. Browse swipe feed
2. Apply filters (amount, category, location, “creators only”)
3. Swipe right on stories that resonate
4. Complete payment (manual first version)
5. Receive confirmation + optional note

## 5. Categories

**Survival**
- Utilities (Meralco, water, internet)
- Rent
- School fees
- Hospital / medical
- Food & essential shopping lists

**Creator Level-Up**
- Specific tools and machines (example: Stark Varg, camera bodies, drones, lighting, editing machines)
- Requires proof of existing competence in the domain

## 6. Hard Rules

- Story is mandatory
- Photo of the real bill or item is mandatory
- Creator Level-Up items require demonstrated current skill
- No forced content creation or public gratitude required
- High-ticket items may have light additional verification
- Platform does not take a cut in the MVP (sustainability model later)

## 7. MVP Feature List

Must have:
- User auth
- Upload form (photo + amount + category + story)
- Swipeable feed
- Basic filters
- Payment confirmation flow (manual)
- Simple status tracking (Pending / Funded / Completed)

Nice to have in v1:
- Voice note support
- Anonymous donor option
- Basic public case study page (opt-in)

## 8. Success Metrics (Early)

- Number of real stories uploaded
- Percentage of stories that get funded
- Average time from upload to funding
- Number of Creator Level-Up items successfully funded
- Organic shares of funded stories

## 9. Non-Goals (for MVP)

- Complex matching algorithms
- Mobile native apps
- Automated payment rails
- Heavy moderation systems
- Social feed / comments
- Gamification

## 10. Design Principles

- Speed over perfection
- Clarity over cleverness
- Dignity over pity
- Proof over promises
- Resonance over scale theater
